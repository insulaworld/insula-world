Hey Research validation

Registers Insula's project agent (ERC-8004) from the dev wallet, then asks Hey Research's validator to confirm on-chain what Insula shipped. Your wallet signs everything; nothing here ever sees a key.

1Connect the dev wallet

It must be the wallet that verified Insula on HEY (0x0f28…84E1). The page switches your wallet to Robinhood Chain.

2Register the Insula World agent

One transaction: register("https://insulaworld.com/agents/insula.json") on the ERC-8004 identity registry. If this wallet already holds the Insula agent, it's found instead of minting a second one.

3Ask Hey to validate

One transaction: validationRequest(Hey's validator, agentId, claim, keccak256(claim)) on the ERC-8004 validation registry. The claim must match Hey's format character for character; confirm it with them.

4Hey's answer

Hey's validator replies on-chain with 100 (shipped in the window) or 0. This checks every 15 seconds.
