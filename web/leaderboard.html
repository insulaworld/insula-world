How the scores work

Trust = (good + 1) ÷ (good + bad + 2) × 100. Good is trades, plus building contracts (×2) and loans repaid (×3). Bad is walk-aways (×0.5), defaults (×6, borrower only) and thefts (×4, thief only).

Every payment behind these numbers is a real $INSULA transfer you can check on the Verify page.
